## 4. Decisions the card didn't make

- **The link's name is given outright (`aria-label`).** With only `aria-hidden` on the letters, Chromium and Playwright compute "i M NST R": each hidden letter is an inline-block, and name computation sets those apart with spaces. The card's "name stays iMNSTR" needed the label.
- **Header wordmark 22 px on phones, 26 on desktop (was 24), eyes 17/20 px, nav gap 8 px on phones (was 14).** With 24 px, the wordmark, eyes and three links wrapped onto two rows at 360; at 22/17/8 they share one row in both languages (measured: 315 of 316 px). Revealed (keyboard focus), the nav drops to a second row; nothing scrolls. At 200% text the eyes wrap under the wordmark (`.brand { flex-wrap }`) instead of overflowing; the hero never wraps.
- **Hero size is fitted to its eyes, not a `19vw` cap:** `--fit-wordmark = (container width − eyes − gap) / 5.5`, with `.wrap` as a size container (`cqi`). 5.5 em is the revealed width in Persian (no letter-spacing); English is 5.2. It stays 50 px at 360 (the design's size) and shrinks only when zoomed (17 px at 180 px, 35 px at 360 with 200% text). Container units have no fallback (Baseline since 2022). At 180 px and 200% text together (400% in effect) it falls to about 2 px; that pair is not in the card's matrix.
- **Podcast empty state: the eyes are above the line, as the card says,** although plate 4's drawing puts them beside it. One class change if the founder prefers the plate.
- **Tap on the hero toggles only for `pointerType` `touch` or `pen`.** A mouse click on the hero is `preventDefault`ed and does nothing (hover shows the letters); the keyboard's Enter does nothing (focus shows them). A tap during the intro takes over: it cancels the intro's timers so the intro can't hide what the tap opened.
- **Intro timers start when the deferred script runs (after parse), not on `load`.** `data-intro` is on `.hero`.
- **Eyes colours are two tokens, the same in dark** (the export keeps `#fbfcf8` and `#1b211d`; only the edge, `--ink`, changes), with system colours in forced colours. Revealed letters inherit `--monster` at weight 500 (the export used ink for the word and green for the letters; the card keeps `--monster`).
- **The draw-in keyframes use `--highlight-size-y`** so dark's full-height fill draws too; the second mark's later timing is `:nth-of-type(2)`. The rule is scoped to `.intro` and `.page-lede--intro`; the podcast lede has no mark, so a test places one to prove the rule is ready.
- **Rows' sweep also transitions colour, 0.35 s,** so in dark the text doesn't turn dark before the highlighter reaches it.
- **Tap areas, measured first** (box, at 360). Failures: inline links in running text 16–18 px tall (the `.empty__more`, `.entry-page__owner` and entry-body links); the Persian "فید" Feed link 41.3 px wide. Fixes: `padding-block: 0.25rem` on those links (an inline box's padding is part of its box and hits, but moves no line), and `min-inline-size: var(--hit)` on `.feed-link`. Everything else passed as built, including the 52 px primary buttons (`.btn--primary`). No target is larger than its box by pseudo-element; the segmented control's radio is absolutely positioned over its option, so the input's box is the area.
- **A link holding a `<time>`, and `.entry-page__date`, count as "date links" at 24 px.** The entry page's date stands alone on its line, which AC-35 words as "inline links inside running text, such as entry dates"; I followed the design README ("inline date links get 24px") and listed it here for review.
- **Entry rows in the admin choose by `@container (min-width: 17rem)`, in rem, not em** (CLAUDE.md says breakpoints are em). Reason in item 5. 17rem is 272 px, the list's width in a 320 px window, where the four columns have always started.
- **Date input:** padding narrows to `--space-sm` under `max-width: 20em`. A DOM check can't read how far the browser draws the digits, so the test guards the padding and keeps the screenshot (`test-results/…/date-at-180.png`); I looked at it: "10/10/2026" and the icon, whole.
- **The sweep:** `expectWithinColumn` is used on admin states only (it asserts at least one control, and public pages have no `button`). "No clipped text" excludes inputs, textareas and selects (they scroll), anything `aria-hidden` or `.visually-hidden`, and names that end in an ellipsis on purpose (B5/B7). Admin states with no address (the composer, the dialog, the failures) get the reduced-motion check in place: counters and the CSS kill-switch, not the pupils, because the script had already bound before the preference changed. Sign-in and setup load again in a fresh reduced-motion context, where the pupils are checked.
- **Beyond the card, each small:** the three pages/markup/asset test groups in `pages.test.ts`, `assets.test.ts` and `house-rules.test.ts`; the "checks catch what they claim to" tests (an injected `img` with no alt, faded text, a 20 px button, a bare link, a clipping box, a field with no edge, a gradient-only `<mark>`, an inline `<style>`, an animation under reduced motion, each must fail its check); `signedInAdmin` carries the CSP watch.
- **Contrast test:** 20 role pairs in both schemes, every hex token must be in a pair or listed as exempt with a reason (so a new colour forces a decision), and the README's quoted numbers pinned to one decimal.

## 5. Where the plan, the card or the brief was wrong, vague or incomplete

- **The card's list of specs that pin the wordmark's text left out `landing.spec.ts`** (`h1` `toHaveText('iMNSTR')`). Fixed; it was the only regression in the regression set.
- **README colour table drift (V-2).** Dark muted `#aab4ad` on the ground is **7.9:1**, not 8.3. The light field edge `#7d837f` is **3.4:1 on the ground** and 3.6:1 on the field's fill; `tokens.css` and the README say 3.6 against the ground. Both comments in `tokens.css` now say what is computed; the README is the reviewer's. Everything else passes with room: the smallest text pair is light muted at 5.7, the smallest boundary pair is the light field edge on the ground at 3.4, dark field edge on the input fill 4.5.
- **The card's "root font 200%" check emulates the text size without changing `em` media queries**, which read the browser's own default size. So at 1280 px it exercises the desktop layout (48em), while a person who has set their browser text to 200% gets the phone layout at 1280 (48em is then 1536 px). The sweep still found a real fault this way (the next item), but step 9 should try the real setting.
- **A real fault at 200% text, 360 px: the admin's entries list ran 120 px off the edge** when a row had a Persian pill (AC-19, AC-36). Cause: the rows' four-column layout was chosen by `@media (min-width: 20em)`, and a window of 360 px is 22.5 of the browser's em whatever the page's own text size is. Fixed with the container query. Found because the sweep seeds a Persian entry, whose row carries a pill; a row with no pill fits.
- **Tap sizes (AC-35) failed on two things the earlier stages' tests never measured** (inline links in sentences; the Persian Feed link). Both fixed above.
- **axe found nothing at WCAG 2.2 AA on any page or state, both languages and both schemes, once these were fixed.** The "checks catch" tests show the harness fails when a page is wrong.
- **Plate 15 against the dark admin:** every colour on the plate's sheet is a dark token already (`#112019`, `#e4e8e3`, `#aab4ad`, `#7f9187`, `#182b22`, `#9aa69f`, `#e8876a`, `#7fd36b`, `#1c3a27`, `#3b1d16`, `#f8d9cd`), and `admin.css` names no colour (a test enforces it). Two things differ and were left, as redesign: the plate draws the three collapsed section rows with 1 px soft rules (alpha .2, token .18), the code draws them with the 1.5 px strong rule except the last, as the light shell plate does; and nothing else.
- **The headers' revealed state rewraps the nav on a phone** (keyboard focus or a forced `.is-revealed`): no overflow, a second row. A design call if wanted otherwise.
- **The hero is smaller when zoomed** (17 px at 180 px) than the old `19vw` cap gave (34 px), because the revealed word and its eyes must fit.
- The plan says the draw-in plays "on the podcast intro"; the lede has no mark until F-2 brings its phrase.
- CD1's `deploy/checks.sh` has no check for the new `wordmark.js`; not asked, so not added (a `HEAD` of the hashed address named in `/` would do).
