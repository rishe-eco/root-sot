## What to build

Plan §5 B9 is the spec. This card settles what it leaves open.

**`assets/wordmark.js`**, plain browser JavaScript, served hashed by `lib/assets.ts` like any asset, loaded with `<script defer src>` by `views/layout.tsx` and by the admin shell in `routes/admin.tsx`. No inline script, no `style` attribute in markup.
- **First line of work:** if `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` matches, return before binding anything (AC-38).
- **Eyes:** one `pointermove` listener on `window`, throttled to one update per animation frame, that finds every `[data-eye]` at move time (so the admin's Preact-drawn eyes follow too) and moves each pupil as the export does. Move pupils through the CSSOM (`el.style.setProperty('--eye-x', …)` or `el.style.transform`), which the CSP allows; never `setAttribute('style', …)`. The blink is CSS: `5.5s infinite`, the second eye 0.04 s behind.
- **Reveal:** the markup carries the hidden letters (i**-**M**o**NST**e**R) as spans with `aria-hidden="true"`, so the link's accessible name stays "iMNSTR". CSS reveals them on `:focus-visible`, on `:hover` inside `@media (hover: hover)`, and under a `.is-revealed` class. JS adds the class for the intro (on, 0.9 s after load; off at 2.9 s; only where the hero carries `data-intro`, i.e. `/` and `/fa/`) and toggles it on a tap **on the landing hero only**: that link is the page you're on, so the tap doesn't reload. In every header the wordmark stays a plain link; a tap navigates.
- **Where the eyes go** (plates 1–4, 6, 13): beside the wordmark in every public header, beside the landing hero, above the empty-state line on `/log`, `/fa/log`, `/podcast`, `/fa/podcast`, and big on both 404s and the admin sign-in (fill B4b's `.eyes-slot`). Other admin headers keep the text wordmark (HIG #9). Every eyes group is `aria-hidden="true"`; replace the sign-in slot's `role="img"` and its label, and drop `copy.signIn.eyes` if nothing else uses it. Sizes come from the export's `pair()` and `Eyes` calls, set in `rem` in `tokens.css`. **Colour:** keep B1's `--monster` wordmark; the revealed letters take weight 500.

**CSS motion** (all of it killed by `base.css`'s reduced-motion rule, which stays):
- the highlighter draw-in on `<mark>` in the landing intro and the podcast intro only, once, right to left in Persian (the export: 0.7 s after 1.2 s, the second mark 0.8 s after 1.8 s, `both`). B7's podcast lede is a plain locale string with no `<mark>`, so scope the rule to it and add no copy (F-2 brings the phrase);
- the big rows' sweep, `0.35s`, replacing B3's instant fill (the comment at `components.css` "arrives in B9");
- the reveal's `max-inline-size` and opacity transition.
