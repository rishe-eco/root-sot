## Never edit app files in a worktree while its e2e runs
**Rule:** In a Tracker review, make no edit under `client/app` in the worktree while a Playwright run against that worktree's Vite dev server is in flight; queue the fix until the run ends, or run its client test only.
**Event:** `verify SR9` committed a locale fix 18 s into `session.spec.ts:532`; Vite reloaded the page, the open row panel closed, and the test hung on a detached "Move down" for its full 180 s. Rerun alone, it passed.
**Cost:** about 4 minutes, and nearly a flake on the record
**Scope:** skill `verify`
**Destination:** `lifecycle/skills/verify/` (step 3) or `lifecycle/projects/tracker/brief-common.md`
**Urgent:** no

## A "take the remaining height" layout needs a floor, checked on a short screen
**Rule:** When a card replaces a fixed minimum height with "the remaining height" (`flex-1 min-h-0`), the card names a floor and the lane measures a short landscape screen (640 × 360) as well as the target size.
**Event:** SR9 item 7 made the calendar fit 360 × 800, and the day list fell from 216 px to 8 px at 640 × 360; the review's probe found it and added `min-h-48`.
**Cost:** a review fix and two tests
**Scope:** project
**Destination:** `lifecycle/projects/tracker/brief-common.md` or `review-checklist.md` §4
**Urgent:** no
