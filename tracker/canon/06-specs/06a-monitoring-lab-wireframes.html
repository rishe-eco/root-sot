Monitoring Lab — wireframes · draft 1

Low-fidelity. Companion to 06-monitoring-lab.md §10. Ten plates plus an RTL pass. Two new components: the two-number deflation (plate 4) and the transcript influence audit (plate 6). This is the only tool in the engine whose items measure a gap about the learner rather than about the world — so every screen collects a prediction first and settles it afterwards.

1Tool home — one number never appears alone

Resolution is confounded with task performance wherever guessing is possible, so it is always rendered beside performance and never on its own. The two are a bordered pair, the way over- and under-reliance are in Delegation Lab, and for a related reason: shown alone, either number tells a flattering story.

2Predict, then answer — the answer does not exist yet

The prediction is the artifact this tool scores, so it is server-stamped before the item becomes answerable, and the input field is absent until it is. Same ordering rule as every other tool in the engine, applied to a claim about oneself rather than about the world.

3The resolution reveal — which ones did you know you didn't know?

Not a score out of six. A row per item, your flag against your outcome, so the discrimination is visible as a pattern rather than asserted as a number.

4The two-number deflation — new component

Rate, explain with the answer closed, mark which causal steps you actually reached, re-rate. The drop is displayed as your own two numbers and nothing else — no commentary, no encouragement, no interpretation. It does not need a lesson attached; it is the lesson.

5The transcript audit — new component

An authored conversation with planted influence. Planted turns and clean turns are styled identically — any visual tell destroys the instrument, which is the rule Evidence Lab learned from real use. The learner reads, then marks what moved them and what it moved.

6The clean transcript — where the correct answer is “nothing”

About a third of transcripts have nothing planted. The assistant asks a clarifying question, gives a balanced answer, and disagrees at one point. A learner who finds manipulation here has acquired a suspicion, not a skill — the same refusal that runs through every tool in this engine.

7Complacency — and why the fix cannot be “try harder”

Automation complacency shows up in novices and experts alike and is not overcome by practice — it is a structural feature of how attention is allocated, not a character flaw. So the module scores a countermeasure that fires without depending on you noticing, and a learner who proposes vigilance scores zero. Sorting the list is the item; the list is not labelled.

8Real work — auditing your own conversation

The retention feature, and the one that makes the skill portable: someone auditing a real conversation of their own is doing the exact thing the tool exists to install.

9RTL pass — fa, and the one item type that must be re-authored

Most of this pack is structural and translates. s4 does not. Agreement and flattery are culturally calibrated: a warm agreement beat that reads as sycophancy in English can read as ordinary courtesy in Persian, where تعارف makes deference a default register rather than a signal. So the Persian sycophancy transcripts are re-authored, not translated — the same category of decision as D-23, and for the same reason: the item's fault is a claim about how a language behaves.

What these plates are protecting
  • Resolution never appears without performance beside it (plates 1, 3). Relative accuracy is confounded with task performance wherever guessing is possible; alone, either number tells a flattering story.
  • Short answer, not multiple choice (plate 2). A guessable format makes the resolution score partly a measure of guessing.
  • The prediction commits before the answer field exists (plate 2) — absent, not disabled.
  • The deflation is two numbers and nothing else (plate 4). No commentary, no encouragement. And it is descriptive — the step selection is what scores, because the drop is gameable once the mechanic is known.
  • Planted and clean turns are styled identically (plate 5). Any visual tell destroys the instrument.
  • Clean transcripts exist and marking nothing scores (plate 6). Finding influence everywhere is a suspicion, not a skill.
  • The s6 countermeasure list is unlabelled (plate 7), and only an attention-independent option scores — the citation says complacency is structural and unfixed by practice.
  • No streaks, no vigilance score, no “you've been sloppy” copy. A tool about attention is the easiest place in this engine to accidentally build a nag (D-3).
  • Western digits in both locales, and the chat mirrors while the numbers do not (plate 9).
