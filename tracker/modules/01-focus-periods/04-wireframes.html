Focus Periods — wireframes · draft 2

Module tracker/modules/01-focus-periods/ · Phase 4 · drawn by hand (the wireframes skill isn't built yet). Low fidelity: layout, content and states only. No brand, type or colour decisions. There is no 03-journeys.md for this module, so frames cite spec sections and acceptance criteria only.

Grading. Every frame is proposal. Captions cite 02-spec.md 0.3: sections (§4.3) and criteria (AC-14). Anything drawn dashed in purple is a Suggested item (G-n): an option, not a requirement. Example data: today is Tue 20 Oct; the active period runs 5–31 Oct, so 12 days are left, counting today. Draft 2 follows the 0.3 change from goals to projects.

1Focus tab — active period

The whole page is the header plus one card per project, in the order you added them. The header gives dates and days left and nothing else: no tally, no colour that shifts near the end. Each card answers two questions: where is this project? (actions done) and what do I do next? (3 actions).

2Project card — complete, reschedule, add, remove

Every management action happens inside the card it belongs to, with one control per effect: the checkbox completes, the day button reschedules, the title navigates. Nothing opens a new page except the titles.

3Focus tab — no period (with the project picker), upcoming, overlap error, loading

The tab is never blank. With no period it explains itself in one line and offers the form. The picker puts projects that already have work scheduled in the chosen dates first, so "the projects tbd in this period" are the first thing you see; you still tick each one yourself.

4Edit period

One sheet for everything about the period itself. Projects are ticked in and out here; their own details are never edited from this page.

5Look-back — the period has ended

Two lists and one optional question. No grade, score, percent or "you finished 2 of 5". The lists are the whole report. The question is the drop rule's measure.

6Persian (فشرده), right to left, at 375 px

The same active period in Persian. Everything mirrors: the checkbox sits on the right, day buttons on the left, the bar fills right to left. Dates are Jalali via useAppDate. The bottom bar carries six items; this plate is the check that Persian labels still fit.

Calls made here

Behaviour these wireframes decided that the spec left open, each proposal. Each one is accepted, refused or carried before the build plan. Numbers are never reused; retired calls stay listed.

#CallWhyWhere
W1"12 days left" is plain text in the date line, the same size as the dates. No big number.A large countdown is the most likely way for the marker to become the motive (R3).plates 1, 6
W2The context line (goal › milestone) is one muted line under the title, cut short with "…" if long.It gives context without competing with the project's own name. The spec decided it should exist (#13); this decides how it looks.plate 1
W3Finished projects stay where they are in the card order, collapsed to "Done".Moving them to the bottom turns the order into a scoreboard.plate 1
W4Retired in draft 2. The "no undone actions, not done" card.A project can't be in that state: with no actions it's "No actions yet", and with all actions done it's "Done".—
W5In the add form, Minutes appears only once a day is picked.The server only needs an estimate for a scheduled action; asking always adds a field most adds don't need.plate 2
W6The ⋯ menu holds exactly two items: Open project, Remove from period. Removing asks once.The page edits the period, not the project.plate 2
W7The picker splits into "Scheduled in these dates" and "Other projects", re-sorts live as the dates change, has a search field, and notes that finished projects aren't listed.It makes "projects tbd in that period" visible without pulling them in automatically (#11).plate 3
W8The overlap error names the conflicting period and its dates, and offers both ways out."Dates overlap" alone leaves you guessing which period.plate 3
W9An upcoming period shows full cards, with working actions, before it starts.Lets you get projects ready before day one; costs nothing extra.plate 3
W10Carried-over projects in the new-period form are marked "carried over".Shows why they're pre-ticked.plate 5
W11Desktop lays cards out in two columns.One column wastes most of a desktop width.plate 1
W12Edit lists the period's projects as ticked rows, with "+ Add projects" opening the full picker, instead of showing the whole picker inline.The edit sheet stays short. The full project list can be long.plates 4, 5
