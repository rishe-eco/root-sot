# Galaxy — intake

*Module `tracker/modules/04-galaxy/` · Track: Module · Phase 0 · 2026-11-19 · Owner: _root*

**Status of this page:** the founder's description, as given. Claims are **proposal** unless marked otherwise. No sources, no research.

---

## 1. Problem

To see what they need, the founder has to go through Tracker's tabs and modules one at a time. No single place shows everything they need to see right now and nothing else. Focus comes closest, but it isn't the interface the founder has in mind, and it needs setting up. Beyond Tracker, software in general makes you work inside options someone else chose and wade through things you never asked for.

**Names** (founder, 2026-11-18). **Galaxy** is this tool: a full-screen room inside Tracker. **Root OS** is the full shell it heads toward, later. The long-term picture is a room that follows you, learns you and reveals itself, then you tell it something and it reveals itself again. Galaxy is the first step: it reveals only from what Tracker already knows.

## 2. Pillar

Organize. Galaxy is a view over Tracker's tree of intent and over the record the session rhythm keeps. Its purpose is **convergence**: to help a life that has scattered in several directions see itself, and slowly line up behind one direction. The founder agreed in principle that this room is where the Journey loop's steps would appear, without leaning on it yet.

## 3. Place on the opportunity tree

Not placed yet. `spec` assigns the node in `ecosystem/ost.md`.

## 4. Who it is for

Someone who uses Tracker on the session rhythm and whose work has spread in several directions. They want one calm place to see their day and their goals, start on the next thing, and then leave the screen for the real world. The founder first.

## 5. Smallest version worth trying

**Interface only.** Galaxy arranges and draws what Tracker and the session rhythm already store (`../../drafts/session-rhythm.md`). Anything that stores or calculates something new is in that draft's section 8b, not here.

- **A full-screen room**, on its own route, with an exit control and nothing else around it. A switch per user, like every non-core feature (session rhythm §10).
- **The opening.** The greeting breaks into the pieces of today, which settle around a center. It's never a step before the day: any click or key skips it, and later opens the same day are shorter.
- **The day,** as the session rhythm's daily view: the plan in order, deep-work spans drawn as bands (Time Themes), crossing out. No counts, no progress, no totals.
- **Needs you:** the derived attention list (§8b), never a count.
- **Tension** drawn as size and glow, once the session rhythm's stage 5b exists.
- **Goals at a glance,** in a general and a specific view. When the session rhythm's stage 5 dashboard exists, this is its face.
- **Panels and gravity.** A piece opens from its point of light and, when closed, returns to its place in orbit. What matters now sits near the center, and the rest drifts out.
- **Session mode.** When a session is due or opened, its steps happen in the room: look-back, then look-ahead. Patterns, including collisions (a tense item that keeps being moved), appear only here.
- **The look.** Dark space with light as information. The ambient layer is alive; the working layer is plain, readable text.

**Out of scope:**
- New data or logic: requirements, tension, deep-work blocks, attention rules, the alignment read. They're in session rhythm §8b.
- Drawing alignment (tilted goal orbits) before the session rhythm's stage 7 can read it.
- Agents running digital work, talking to the room, and the room learning you. These are later turns of the reveal loop.
- Root OS as a shell, and the business branch (`ecosystem/working/root-os-business-horizon.md`).
- Replacing Tracker's other modules.
- Counts, scores, totals, red, overdue marks, streaks, and history charts.
- Building in the same round as the session rhythm. Galaxy is built after the rhythm's stage 1 gate; its design can keep going on probes until then.

The opening probe (`ecosystem/working/root-os-probe/01-opening.html`, 0.2) predates the session rhythm check. Three things in it go against the rhythm and aren't carried forward: counts ("Two things want you", "3 need attention"), red for collisions, and collisions shown in the daily room.

## 6. What would make us drop it

> Drop it if I go back to the plain day list to actually get work done, because Galaxy is prettier than it is useful.

*The founder chose this wording from three that Claude proposed (2026-11-19).*

**Testable:** yes, if Tracker can tell which surface the person works from: Galaxy or the plain daily view. `eval-plan` names the measure.
