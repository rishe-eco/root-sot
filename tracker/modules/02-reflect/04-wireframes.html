Tracker · Reflect — wireframes · draft 1

Module tracker/modules/02-reflect/ · Phase 4 · wireframes. Low fidelity: layout, content and states only. No brand, type or colour decisions; those are step 4b. Every step of 03-journeys.md 0.2 is drawn here or listed in Coverage as having no screen.

Grading. Every frame is proposal. Captions cite journey steps (J1.7), spec sections (§4.2) and acceptance criteria (AC-15) from 02-spec.md 0.1. Anything drawn dashed in purple is a Suggested item (G-n, journeys §4): an option for revise, not a requirement; G1–G4 are founder decisions [F] not yet in the spec. Right-to-left frames are Persian (dir="rtl"); placeholder bars stand for Persian keys not yet written. Practice content (prompts, palettes, catch copy) is English in both locales (§2 #7); where it appears in a Persian frame it is drawn left to right. The calm-close copy is the founder's to approve (R6) and is drawn as bars.

neutral notice success error / not done Suggested (G-n), option only placeholder text

1Tools home — the Reflect card, in English and Persian

Reflect is a third card beside Feelings & Needs and Noticing, the same shape as theirs: one line and one button. Nothing about entries or how many there are.

2Reflect home — loading, first day, with past entries, an entry open, and Persian with the banner

The home holds one decision: start, or continue the one that's open. Past entries is a quiet link once one exists. Nothing on it counts.

3Pre-check — default, the advisory, and Persian

Two optional questions and Next. The advisory doesn't block: it replaces Next with two choices on the same screen. Stop here takes its place in the step bar here, where it stays on every step.

4Story — writing, past the soft line, not saved, at the bound, session expired, and Persian

Two fields and the observer prompt. Saving is silent while it works (W6); only a failure is shown. Nothing is offered while the person writes.

5Feelings and needs — loading, a first moment, the faux-feeling offer, three moments, and Persian

Moments one at a time; the open one is a card, earlier ones fold to a line (W7). The catch is an offer under the feeling it's about, inside that moment.

6Reality check — loading, the offer, after Use this, no offer, and Back

The story comes back as the person wrote it, folded on a phone. At most one offer, above the pairs, and it acts only on itself. The story is never marked up (W9).

7Re-reading close — filled, and left empty in Persian

One field, and Finish in Next's place. Finish works filled or not.

8After the entry and done — the question, the done line, and the entry URL with nothing open

One tap or Skip, then a done line that says nothing about how many. No Stop here: the entry is already closed.

9Calm close — after Not now with nothing written, after Stop here, and the delete confirm

The same screen after Not now and after Stop here. Back to Reflect is the main way out; deleting is quieter, below it (W13). Its copy is the founder's (R6).

10Past entries — loading, one row in Persian, two rows, and empty

Date and scene, newest first. The whole row is the only control. No count, search, filter or grouping.

11One past entry — finished in Persian, stopped, the delete confirm, and not found

Read-only, in step order, with only what was written. Delete sits at the end of the entry (W15).

Calls made here

Behaviour these wireframes decided that the spec and journeys left open, each proposal. ux-review and build-plan's gate read this table: each call is accepted, refused or carried before the build plan.

#CallWhyWhere
W1Stop here sits at the end of a step bar at the top of every entry step, pre-check included, as a small outlined control.§4.9 asks for the same place on every step; the top bar is the one place no step's content pushes around, and the top is reachable without scrolling past a long story.plates 3–7
W2Back at the bottom start, Next (or Finish) at the bottom end. No Back on the pre-check; Back elsewhere is G2's.One footer shape for every step; Back and Next never swap sides, and they mirror in Persian.plates 3–7
W3Each step is named in the bar; there's no step counter or progress bar."2 of 5" is a number on screen (AC-44) and reads as a task to get through.plates 3–7
W4The pre-check's forward button is Next, with any answers or none. The advisory appears on the pre-check under the questions as a trigger answer is tapped, and replaces Next with Not now and Keep going; clearing the answer brings Next back.§4.3 says "before the story" but not where. On the same screen it can't be missed and needs no extra screen.plate 3
W5While the home loads, a placeholder holds the button's place; Start and Continue appear only once the server answers.A flash of Start before Continue invites a tap that closes the open entry.plate 2
W6Autosave shows nothing while it works; only a failure shows (G3).A "saving… saved" mark flickers beside the hardest text and adds noise; the failure line is the one that matters.plate 4
W7One moment open at a time as a card; earlier moments fold to a one-line summary (when · feeling · need) that reopens on tap. Next with no moment skips the step; no separate Skip button.Three open cards don't fit a phone; §4.5 says the step can be skipped without saying how.plate 5
W8The faux-feeling offer sits inside its moment, under the feeling. Use one then a tap on a hint replaces the feeling.§4.5 "under that moment"; inside the card keeps it attached to the feeling it's about.plate 5
W9The shown-back story is folded to its first three lines on a phone with Show all; a tap anywhere on it toggles. The trigger word is not marked in the story; the offer names it.§4.6 gestures: the story is never a control beyond expanding. Marking the word in the person's own text reads as a correction.plate 6
W10After Use this, focus moves to that pair's camera half.It's the next thing to write; J1.11 has B write it at once.plate 6
W11A reading pair stacks reading above camera on a phone, each under its own label.The two labels are long; side by side they truncate at 375 px.plate 6
W12The after-entry answers are three equal full-width buttons in the spec's order; Skip is a text link below. The screen has no Stop here or Back.No answer looks preferred; the entry is already closed.plate 8
W13On the calm close, Back to Reflect is the main button and Delete what I wrote a text link below it.Keeping is the default the copy promises; deleting stays one deliberate step away.plate 9
W14A list row is the date above the scene line, the scene cut to one line.Dates scan down the left edge; rows stay one height.plate 10
W15The entry view labels each part with a short interface string (Scene, What happened, Moments, …), has a link back to Past entries at the top, and Delete at the end of the entry.The practice prompts are too long as headings; Delete after the content, not beside the title, so it isn't the first thing reached.plate 11
W16Continue carries the start date in its own label; Start a new one is secondary, under it.§4.2's wording; one button, not a sentence plus a button. Continue is the safer tap.plate 2
W17Both ConfirmDialogs say it can't be undone, with Cancel and Delete; Cancel at the start.AC-16 and AC-40 require the sentence; the labels follow the app's dialog.plates 9, 11
W18Past entries is a text link under the main button, not a card.A quiet way back in, never a tally or a shelf to fill.plate 2
W19The draft-locale banner has no dismiss control on the home.§4.2 requires it whenever the condition holds; a dismissed banner can't meet that.plate 2
