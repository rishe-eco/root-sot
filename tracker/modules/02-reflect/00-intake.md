## 1. Problem

A minimum version of the Reflect pillar of Journey, built inside Tracker. Someone who is stuck in a pattern replays an experience in their head and spirals, and has no place that helps them re-see it. Tracker's Journals and Feelings & Needs record and name things, but neither walks a person from the story to a clearer reading of it.

*The audience and problem are Claude's proposal, accepted by the founder.*

## 2. Pillar

Reflect (`ecosystem/canon/02-pillars/reflect.md`, 0.2). The Loop's entry point for this person is "stuck in a pattern → Reflect".

## 3. Place on the opportunity tree

Not placed yet. `spec` assigns the node in `ecosystem/ost.md`.

## 4. Who it is for

Someone who comes back to the same experience again and again without getting anywhere with it. They want to put what happened into words and see it more truly, not log it, score it or plan around it.

## 5. Smallest version worth trying

- **One entry at a time**, with structured prompts only. No AI and no agent mirror.
- **Story first:** what happened, in the person's own words: a day, an hour, a moment.
- **Feelings and needs within the story:** name where feelings arose and which needs were met or unmet, anchored to the story.
- **Reality check:** separate what happened (observation) from the reading of it (interpretation).

**Out of scope:**
- The AI or agent mirror.
- Cross-entry pattern recognition or comparison of any kind. It is gated on psychological review (`reflect.md` §4).
- Scores, scales, ratings, streaks or any other quantification.
- The hand-off to Learn, Organize or Impact, and the "act now" exit.
- Deferred tasks, reminders or intervals produced by Reflect.
- Sharing entries with other people.

## 6. What would make us drop it

> one and two

*Said in answer to two options Claude offered: (1) drop if people finish an entry no differently than if they had just journaled, with no re-seeing, or feel more caught in it (the reconstrual bet, H6b, fails); (2) drop if it makes anyone ruminate more or feel scored or judged.*

**Testable:** no, because both are self-reports with no instrument yet, and "no differently than journaling" needs a comparison. `spec` and `eval-plan` make them testable.
