## 4. Decisions the card didn't make

1. **`npm ci --legacy-peer-deps` for the client.** A plain `npm install` or `npm ci` fails with ERESOLVE on the client's peer ranges. CI uses `--legacy-peer-deps` (ci.yml:58). `brief-common.md` says `npm install`.
2. **Next on the story moves to FEELINGS,** as B5 will need it; the entry page renders the bar and Stop here for FEELINGS, REALITY and CLOSE and no body (`{/* B5 plugs in here */}`). Until B5, a person who taps Next on the story reaches a step with only Stop here. Accepted because the routes are unlinked (PD-5). The alternative, a disabled Next, would have had B5 undo it.
3. **The story's two fields are labelled with the pack's `scenePrompt` and `storyPrompt`.** Plate 4 draws a second label, "What happened, in order.", above the observer prompt. The pack has no such string and §6.5 makes the prompts practice content, so I used the prompt as the label and added no key.
4. **Failure lines.** A failed autosave shows `reflect.notSaved` (G3). A failed pre-check commit shows the same line. A failed move or stop (text already saved) shows a different line, `reflect.errors.action`, with Try again, since "isn't saved yet" would be false there. Try again re-runs the last action. A failed delete shows `reflect.errors.delete` under the calm close.
5. **A pre-check commit failure holds Next, Keep going, Not now and Stop here** until Try again, the same as G3's rule for a failed autosave.
6. **Past the bound, the soft-line sentence gives way to the bound sentence** (one sentence at a time, AC-20/AC-51).
7. **`useReflectAutosave` also flushes on unmount,** best effort, so leaving the page with text waiting still sends it.
8. **All saves go through one ordered queue** so two saves of one field can't land out of order. The card says only that each field's pause is 800 ms.
9. **The banner on the entry page** is state: it shows until the first move, then goes (G17 "first step shown in a sitting"). It is not shown on the calm close. The entry page reads `locale` and `reviewStatus` from `reflectContent`; the home reads them from `reflectHome`.
10. **The delete goes to the home with `replace: true`,** so Back doesn't return to a dead entry route. Start (home to entry) is a plain push.
11. **Copy I wrote** (all marked for founder review at B7 for the calm close; the rest is mine): `en` and `fa` for the home, pre-check, bound, soft-line, not-saved and error lines. The `fa` calm close is draft like the `en`. I used بازتاب for Reflect and نوشته for entry; neither is in the conventions glossary (7c).
12. **Canon this change makes stale:** none in `01-data-model.md` or the decision log (no schema). `03-frontend.md` (screens and routes) and `02-graphql-api.md` don't list Reflect's client; both are for the reviewer or `close-out` to update. `07-build-plan.md` §5 B4's "evicted from the Apollo cache" (item 5, below).

## 5. Where the plan, card or brief was wrong, vague or incomplete

1. **D-9's eviction, "the Apollo cache".** Nothing Reflect reads goes through Apollo (`useApi` keeps nothing), so there is nothing to evict. Met by construction, as the card says; there is a comment at the delete site in `ReflectEntryPage.tsx`. **For verify:** if any later Reflect read uses an Apollo hook, the delete must evict. The plan's sentence should become "no Reflect read goes through Apollo".
2. **`brief-common.md` setup says `npm install` for the client;** it fails with ERESOLVE. CI uses `--legacy-peer-deps`.
3. **Past entries link goes to `/tools/reflect/past`, which has no route until B6.** With `hasPast` true (any closed entry with a story) the link shows and leads to the app's not-found. Unlinked from the Tools home, so only a person typing the URL reaches it. No test covers it.
4. **`hasStory` is story text only,** so G4's line says "simply ends" for an entry holding a scene, moments or readings but no story, and that entry is kept in Past entries once B6 lists it (AC-37). The card already flags it (B6 redefines it); not B4's to fix.
5. **Plate 4's second label** (decision 3) and **plate 9's closing lines** (the card says draft copy; plate 9 shows three lines I split into `fine`, `kept`, `support`).
6. **The card's e2e list says "a second start → story → Stop here".** "Second" reads as a second entry; I took it as a second walk (a fresh entry after the first was closed by Not now).
7. **`moveReflectEntry` returns `interpretationOffer`;** B4's document selects `entry` only. B5 widens the document when it surfaces the offer.
8. **The `fa` pre-check question words** are mine, informal register, and have not had a Persian reader.
