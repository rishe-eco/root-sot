## 4. Decisions the card didn't make

1. **Hints are the offer's feeling hints only.** The card says *Use one* then a hint saves that hint as the feeling. The offer also carries `needHints`; I show none of them (AC-25 and PD-9 speak of the feeling only), and the offer's `dismiss` text is not used: *Use one* and *Keep mine* are the wireframes' interface strings, `reflect.faux.useOne` and `reflect.faux.keepMine`, not F&N's "leave it — that word fits". `note` is shown small under the offer. The `fa` interface sees English hints and `note`, as G8 says. Say if the need hints should replace the need too.
2. **A hint's source (PD-9)** compares case-insensitively with the served feelings labels after stripping a trailing `?` or `؟`. The card said "equals a label"; case folding is mine.
3. **Own-word storage.** The held value of an own word is `{ text, source: "OWN" }`. Clearing a word sends `feeling: ""` (stored as null) and leaves the stored source as it was.
4. **`SaveValue` is an object for a word with its source.** The autosave hook's value type became `string | { text, source } | null`, so a feeling and its source travel as one queue entry and can't be split by a reordering.
5. **The dimmed-pairs state** (plate 6, "loading") is a page flag set while the move into the reality check is out. The Reality step renders at once with its fields disabled and `aria-busy`, and Next, Back and Stop here are held.
6. **Use this when no reading is empty.** If every pair has a reading and the count is under three and every pair is stored, a new pair is added and filled. If it cannot be placed (full at three, or a pair not yet stored), the offer is dismissed and nothing is filled. A sentence longer than the reading bound (280) is cut at 280 without splitting a surrogate pair.
7. **Shown-back story folds at every width**, not only on a phone, and *Show all* is always offered for a non-empty story. I did not measure overflow to hide the toggle. The toggle is a real button inside the box; its click bubbles to the box's handler, so there is one toggle, not two (tested).
8. **Reading pairs and moments carry no visible number.** Several pairs share the same labels; for assistive technology they are told apart by order only. AC-44 argued against a visible number.
9. **Footer wraps.** `ReflectFooter` and `ReflectStepBar` are now `flex-wrap`. The step bar change was needed (180 px, item 3); the footer change is a precaution with no failing case.
10. **The answer buttons' lock lives in the page**, not in the question component, so there is one lock and the page is what calls the server. An answer that fails to store does not hold the person on the question (the answer is optional): the page moves to the done line anyway and shows no error.
11. **Done line and after-entry copy are my drafts** (`reflect.after.*`). The plate has no question text, so none is shown. The `fa` strings (with the Finish, Show all and the three answers) are informal and need P0-2's reader. The calm close is untouched.
12. **Slot ports.** Slot A's ports were taken (item 6), so I ran e2e on 4030 and 5203, outside the excluded ranges. Nothing else changed in config.

    - `02-architecture/02-graphql-api.md`: the client documents and what each selects (Reflect's four new operations; the moment and content widening); and `saveReflectMoment`'s null rule for `met`.
    - `02-architecture/03-frontend.md`: the three new steps, the keyed autosave (`hold`, `commit`), and the page's history-state rule.

## 5. Where the plan, card or brief was wrong, vague or incomplete

1. **"Plan says client only"** (card, *Met can't be cleared today*): B2's null rule meant AC-11 and "clearable" couldn't both hold. One API line and one integration test; the plan's "client only" for B5 is wrong by that line. This is the flag the card asked for.
2. **`otherLabel` is "other → type it"**, not "other…" (served by `reflectContent`; plate 5 and the card draw "other…"). My component tests use "other…" from the fixture; the e2e uses the real label.
3. **The wireframes call the reality-check heading "Reading it back"**; the code's step name (B4's `reflect.steps.reality`) is "Reality check", and the e2e asserts that.
4. **The offer's `sentence` has no closing stop**: the server splits on `.`, `!`, `?` and line breaks, so `"She always does this when we visit."` comes back as `"She always does this when we visit"`. The card says "fills with the sentence"; the filled reading carries no full stop. The e2e asserts that form. Correct if the stop should be kept (a B3 change).
5. **Slot A's ports were not free.** `4010` was held by an API (`ts-node src/index.ts`, started 17:18) and `5183` by a Vite server (started 17:14), both run from `E:\_root\tracker\.claude\worktrees\reflect-b4-entry-shell`: the B4 lane's servers, left running. I did not stop them (another lane's worktree), and I ran slot-A-shaped e2e on 4030 and 5203, which I confirmed free. They are probably still up.
6. **The 180 px reflow check found a B4 defect**: `ReflectStepBar` put Stop here outside the column beside a long step name ("Feelings and needs", "Reality check", "Looking at it now"). B4's review measured "the story, the pre-check and the calm close" only, where the names are short. Fixed here with `flex-wrap`.
7. **`reflect.nav.finish` was not an existing key**; B4's `nav` has only `next`, `back` and `stop`. Added.
8. **The card's "J1 ... both offers" implies both surface on one entry.** They do, because they are per-entry, per-kind and separate cooldowns (a new user each run). The e2e uses a trigger in the story ("always") and the feeling "ignored".
