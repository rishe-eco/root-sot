## 4. Decisions the card didn't make

1. The offer helpers live in `catches.ts` and take the entry row. `entry.ts` stays thin. They are named `interpretationOfferFor` and `fauxOfferFor`, so the fence's forbidden names appear nowhere in Reflect.
2. A missing `ReflectState` returns no offer rather than throwing. The card says to update it, and `startReflectEntry` always ensures it, so this can't happen through the API. The offer writes use `update`, as the card says.
3. A feeling saved as `""` normalizes to null and is never matched.
4. `word` is the first-compiled trigger among equals. A tie on weight across two spellings returns the one listed first, and the earlier sentence wins a tie.
5. The imports fence resolves each specifier to a path and judges the path, so `../noticing/x`, `services/noticing/x` and `require`/`import()` are all caught. It also rejects a default, namespace or `export *` import of distinctions.
6. The AC-26 fence also reports an import of `services/noticing` by a bare directory (`../noticing`).
7. Stale canon: none from a schema (none changed). `02-reflect/07-build-plan.md` §5 B3 says "`maybeCatch` or `catchAllowed`" are never used, and the fence now holds it. The development README's stage list needs a B3 line (the reviewer's).
