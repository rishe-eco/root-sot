D-XX+1 · Identity core amended: beauty as telos — DECIDED (provisional pending field test)
Decided: 2026-07-14 · Owner: _root · Status: locked, one revisit conditionDecision. Beauty joins the identity core as the direction of self-authorship. The thesis is now: "The person authors their own life — in search of beauty. Root never authors it for them." Master tagline pair: "Root — in search of beauty" / «ریشه — در جست‌وجوی زیبایی», operating under the two-tier system (an orienting descriptor pays the clarity bill; the tagline is the compass, not the map).Rationale. Sole frame found that umbrellas the full growth map (coaching, therapy, communication, peacebuilding, education) without instrumentalizing any of it; enacts the values-defined audience by declaring Root's direction and letting fellow travelers self-select; unsayable by the engagement economy. Origin: authored by the founder, not generated — which is itself on-thesis.Known risk. The salon trap: root + beauty reads haircare/cosmetics in English commercial space; زیبایی gravitates toward سالن/جراحی زیبایی in Persian. Mitigation: never let the tagline appear naked — descriptor adjacent, restrained visual identity carrying it (set in Apple-air it reads philosophy; in swirly serif it reads shampoo). Synonym escapes are closed: حسن and جمال both collapse into men's names.Revisit condition. Field test with people outside the team's context. The test is a category-guess test, not an approval test: show the name + tagline pair cold, ask "what does this company do?" If a meaningful share of both Persian and English speakers guess beauty/cosmetics, the line moves from master tagline to manifesto interior and we hunt again. Liking is not the metric; mis-categorization is.Downstream effects. Brand definition core section rewrites around the amended thesis. Pillar framing inherits an aesthetic vocabulary option (Reflect as seeing clearly, Others/Serve as adding beauty to other lives — to be worked, not assumed). The dual register of Beauty (fully secular reading public; deeper resonance private) is intentional and stays uncommented in public documents.
