## 2. The anatomy template

Every process is dissected on the same eight dimensions, so they stay comparable and the format is reusable for later modules.

| Dimension | What it captures | What it powers |
|---|---|---|
| **The move** | what the person actually does / experiences | design fidelity |
| **Mechanism** | the causal chain — *why* the move produces the effect (the cognitive/affective lever) | knowing what's load-bearing vs. decorative |
| **System / coupling** | where it sits in the loop; upstream dependency; what it feeds downstream | sequencing; not breaking a process by moving it |
| **Preconditions** | what must be true for it to fire at all | onboarding, gating, edge cases |
| **Failure modes** | how it breaks or backfires | guardrails |
| **Observable signals** | what you'd see when it's working | **evaluation** (and the discovery coding sheet) |
| **Dials** | the tunable parameters | **the evaluation-and-settings layer** (`learn.md` §3 keeps deferring to this) |
| **Evidence & grade** | link to `evidence-summary`, with strong / moderate / thin | defending the choice; knowing the soft spots |

The last two are where most of the leverage is: **Observable signals** is the seed of per-process evaluation (an open question in `learn.md` §9), and **Dials** is the concrete content of the settings layer the module keeps hardcoding "for the prototype."

## 4. The system — how the processes compose

The processes are not a list; they're a coupled system, which is what "what system are they taking place in" was really asking.

- **P1 (mindset) is the substrate for everything** — "mindset before vocabulary" (`learn.md` §2). It gates the rest and is *reinforced by* every successful pass of them (each landed name is another data point that emotion is workable). It's the one process that is both upstream of all and downstream of all.
- **The bare loop is P2→P3→P4** in sequence: attend to the body (P2) → reach for a word (P3) → read it as a need (P4). This is one ~3-minute motion, not three steps the user experiences separately.
- **P5 (distinctions) is a refinement layer on P3/P4** — "B is a refinement on top of A, never the opening move" (`learn.md` §2). It fires *in-context*, catching the person's own faux-feelings, not as a drill.
- **P6 (reconstrual) is the top of the spiral and the door into Reflect** — it operates on whole moments and *is* a guided Reflect entry. It's the load-bearing integration bet (H6b).
- **P7 (self-initiation) is the exit condition, not a step** — when the loop runs unprompted, the scaffold has installed the capacity.
- **P8 sits orthogonal:** it modulates *whether P2 can even fire* (under threat, interoceptive access narrows). That coupling is exactly why it's a sibling, not a sub-step of Module 1.

```
                 ┌───────────────── P1 mindset (substrate; reinforced by all) ─────────────────┐
                 │                                                                              │
   [a felt moment] → P2 body → P3 word → P4 need → (small action) ──── the bare loop ───────────┘
                                  ↑P5 distinctions refine, in-context
                        P6 storytelling reconstrues whole moments → door into Reflect
                        P7 loop self-initiates = graduation
   P8 (situational load) ── modulates whether P2 can fire at all ── future sibling module
```

## 5. Process anatomies

### P1 — Malleability-mindset installation  *(worked)*

- **The move.** The Day-1 frame (`learn.md` §3, ~6 min, once) plus every subsequent loop: the person *experiences* that naming a feeling gives them a handle on it, though nothing else changed — installing the **incremental** (vs. entity) stance toward emotion.
- **Mechanism.** Implicit theories of emotion (Tamir; adjacent to Dweck). Believing emotion is malleable → greater willingness to *attempt* regulation → better regulation outcomes. The belief updates from **experience, not assertion** — an enactive update ("I felt the handle appear"), which is precisely why Day-1 is designed as *felt, not told.* Telling someone emotions are workable is a slogan; letting them feel it once is a belief change.
- **System / coupling.** The foundational substrate. No upstream dependency. Gates vocabulary (mindset before A). Uniquely, it is *reinforced by every other process* — each successful loop is another malleability data point — so it both precedes and is fed by P2–P7.
- **Preconditions.** A genuine **early felt win** — hence the palette is weighted to easy / pleasant / met-need moments first (`learn.md` §3). Without an early success the belief fails to install or *dis*confirms.
- **Failure modes.** (a) Asserting malleability instead of eliciting it → reads as a motivational slogan, can backfire. (b) Early loops that fail to land any word → actively teach the opposite (emotions are opaque and fixed).
- **Observable signals.** Language shift from *"that's just how I am / it takes over"* → *"I can work with this"*; self-initiated attempts to influence a feeling. **This is verbatim H4's evidence signal** — mechanism doc and discovery coding sheet already align.
- **Dials.** Number of easy-win loops before harder material; how explicitly the frame is stated vs. purely elicited; how early "unpleasant/unmet-need" words enter the palette.
- **Evidence & grade.** Implicit theories of emotion — **moderate–strong**; `evidence-summary` §5 calls it *"the single best-evidenced lever for the module."* This is why the whole module is frame-first, and why narrowing Module 1 to "a dictionary" would discard its strongest component.

### P2 — Interoceptive on-ramp  *(worked)*

- **The move.** Breath, then attend to *where / what texture* the state has in the body (tight, heavy, jittery, warm) **before** reaching for a word (`learn.md` §3, the bare loop's first move).
- **Mechanism.** Interoception / focusing (Gendlin; alexithymia literature). Body-awareness is **upstream of naming**: attending to sensation, then testing words against it, gives a **non-analytic** entry to the feeling. Two effects: it grounds the eventual label in something real (not a story *about* the feeling), and it **keeps attention off the "why am I like this" cognitive loop** — i.e. it structurally sidesteps rumination rather than relying on willpower to avoid it.
- **System / coupling.** First move of the bare loop; upstream of P3 (word) and P4 (need). Depends on nothing except the capacity to attend inward — **which is exactly what P8 (situational load) can suppress.** Downstream, it improves the *quality* of the label P3 reaches for.
- **Preconditions.** An actual felt moment to attend to; **enough safety/calm to attend inward** (the precondition P8 threatens). For high-alexithymia users the sensation itself may be faint — the on-ramp has to tolerate "I don't feel anything yet." *(Build note: the daily loop opens with a short skippable **breath beat** — this precondition made into a step; see `../learn-build/00-module1-demo-plan.md` §4.2. Kept a settling threshold, never a timer/meditation.)*
- **Failure modes.** (a) Jumping straight to the story, bypassing the body — the H5 deficit itself. (b) Dwelling on sensation so long it *becomes* analysis/spiral — violates "naming is not analyzing" (`learn.md` §5). (c) Blank at "where do you feel it" — must be handled as information, not failure.
- **Observable signals.** Can locate and describe a sensation with a texture word (tight/heavy/buzzy/hot) vs. going blank, finding the question strange, or jumping to narrative. **Maps to H5's evidence signal.**
- **Dials.** How long to dwell on sensation before reaching for a word; how much the body-scan is scaffolded vs. open; size of the texture palette; whether "I feel nothing" gets its own gentle branch.
- **Evidence & grade.** Interoception / focusing — **moderate** (`evidence-summary` §5). Well-motivated theoretically (body-awareness upstream of naming; non-analytic on-ramp), thinner on hard outcome trials than affect labeling itself.

### P3 — Granularity-in-context  *(worked)*

- **The move.** In the bare loop, reach for a *specific* feeling word and **try it on** against the felt sense from P2 — distinguishing nearby feelings (disappointed vs. drained vs. uneasy), from a small palette weighted to pleasant/met-need early. Across many repetitions the discriminations sharpen.
- **Mechanism.** Emotional granularity (Barrett): finer-grained emotional categories enable better-targeted regulation and less maladaptive coping. The *training* mechanism is **repeated in-context labeling** — each time you pin a word to a live felt state, you refine that category's boundary. The active ingredient is the **word-to-sensation pairing** (which is why P2 has to come first): a word tested against a felt referent becomes a usable category; a word learned off a list stays a dictionary entry. "Try it on" *is* the category-refinement act.
- **System / coupling.** Middle of the bare loop — downstream of P2 (the body supplies the referent the word is tested against), upstream of P4 (the named feeling is then read as a need). Hard dependency on P2: a word with no felt referent collapses into memorization (the failure mode). Reinforces P1 each time a word lands; P5 refines on top of it.
- **Preconditions.** A live felt referent from P2. A palette that is *present but small* — large enough to stretch past "good/bad/fine/stressed," small enough not to become a menu. The word must be one the person can *own and use*, not merely recognize.
- **Failure modes.** (a) Teaching it as a word-list / flashcard drill → recognition without granularity — the exact "oversell vocabulary size" trap (`learn.md` §5); words that never attach to felt states. (b) Palette too large → browsing a menu, which breaks the "keep moving" loop. (c) Forcing a "correct" word → the person defers to the app's taxonomy instead of building their own — off-pillar by the §8 decision test (teaching a taxonomy to recite).
- **Observable signals.** Reaches, unprompted, for a specific word beyond "good/bad/fine/stressed"; distinguishes nearby feelings; uses words they've *used in context*, not just picked from the palette. **Maps to H1's evidence signal.** Over time the palette is leaned on less — which is also a P7 graduation signal.
- **Dials.** Palette size and composition; the pleasant/met-need early weighting vs. when to broaden; exposures before a word counts as "owned"; whether the app ever *offers* a word or always waits for the person.
- **Evidence & grade.** Emotional granularity — **strong** on the correlation with better coping, **moderate** on trainability (`evidence-summary` §5). Load-bearing caveat: **granularity ≠ vocabulary size** (distinct constructs) — the payoff is discrimination built in context, never word-count.

### P4 — Feeling→need coupling  *(worked)*

- **The move.** After naming the feeling, read it as *information about a need* — "this points at something I care about; what?" The need is **offered, never forced** (`learn.md` §3). Naming and need-orientation become one motion, not two steps.
- **Mechanism.** The design axiom that feeling and need are **two ends of one mechanism** — the feeling is the signal, the need is what it signals about (`learn.md` §2). Coupling gives the label a *referent that points toward action/meaning* instead of dead-ending as a description. Why it beats teaching feelings alone: a gauge you can read but whose measurand you don't know yields no move; coupling supplies the move. Empirical weight sits on **affect labeling** (naming down-regulates — strong) plus **SDT's needs** (autonomy/competence/relatedness as what feelings signal about — strong).
- **System / coupling.** Third move of the bare loop — downstream of P3 (needs a real label), upstream of the optional small action (the seam into Reflect). Coarse labels point nowhere, so **P3's quality gates P4.** Feeds P5 (need-vs-strategy refines it), P6 (storytelling surfaces plural/competing needs), and the small-action seam. Reinforces P1.
- **Preconditions.** A named feeling specific enough to point somewhere. The need offered as a *candidate*, held lightly — the "never forced" is a precondition, not politeness: a forced need is one the person recites rather than recognizes.
- **Failure modes.** (a) Forcing the need → the person recites the app's need taxonomy (off-pillar). (b) Collapsing need into strategy at this stage ("I need him to text back") — P5's job to catch, but P4 can seed it by rushing. (c) Presenting this as NVC doctrine rather than a light coupling move → leans the weight onto a thin framework.
- **Observable signals.** Spontaneously moves from "I feel X" to "…because I needed/wanted Y"; treats a feeling as informative rather than merely unpleasant. Adjacent to the H2/H3 signals (though the *distinctions* there are P5's).
- **Dials.** How strongly the need is prompted vs. left open; whether a need palette is offered at all; how early coupling carries weight (present from the bare loop, but tunable).
- **Evidence & grade.** Affect labeling + SDT — **strong**; **NVC as design language only — thin** (`evidence-summary` §6), flag explicitly. The coupling *concept* is well-grounded; its NVC dressing is *not* the evidence and must not be presented as if it were.

### P5 — Distinction-by-contrast  *(worked)*

- **The move.** When the person produces a faux-feeling ("ignored," "let down," "disrespected"), the app **gently catches it in-context**: that's a read on what someone *did* — underneath is the feeling (hurt? lonely?) and the need (to matter? to be included?). Two distinctions: feeling-vs-thought and need-vs-strategy. Always on the person's own live material, never a standalone drill (`learn.md` §3).
- **Mechanism.** Variation theory / contrasting cases: concept boundaries are learned from **minimal pairs** — you learn what a feeling *is* by contrasting it with the near-miss (a judgment masquerading as a feeling). Two things make it work: the contrast is drawn on the person's *own* material *at the moment they produce it*, so the boundary is salient exactly where it operates; and the **"gentle catch, not a quiz" is a mechanism requirement, not a tone preference** — a quiz triggers defensiveness and abstract, performative processing; a catch keeps the person inside the live material where the contrast actually lands.
- **System / coupling.** A **refinement layer on P3/P4** — "B on top of A, never the opening move" (`learn.md` §2). Fires opportunistically (~week 2, distributed touches), *triggered* by the person producing a faux-feeling or a need-as-strategy. Depends on A being underway (you can't refine a distinction the person isn't yet drawing). Feeds P6, whose first step — separating observation from evaluation — *is* this distinction at whole-moment scale.
- **Preconditions.** The person must produce their *own* faux-feeling / strategy-as-need for there to be something to catch — so it is live-triggered, not scheduled. Enough A-competence that a refinement has something to refine.
- **Failure modes.** (a) Teaching it as a standalone drill/quiz → defensiveness, abstraction, off-pillar recital. (b) Correcting too hard → the person feels caught out and disengages. (c) Firing before A is established → B as the opening move (explicitly forbidden). (d) Canned examples instead of the person's material → the contrast doesn't land where it matters.
- **Observable signals.** Catches at least *some* of their own faux-feelings and need/strategy confusions in the moment; finds the feeling under "ignored," the need under "I need him to call." **Maps to H2 (feelings-as-judgments) and H3 (need-vs-strategy) evidence signals.**
- **Dials.** How gentle vs. explicit the catch; frequency of the distributed touches; how much the app scaffolds the "underneath" vs. lets the person find it; the threshold for when to catch vs. let a faux-feeling pass.
- **Evidence & grade.** Variation theory / contrasting cases — **moderate**, well-established in instruction research (`evidence-summary` §5). The NVC faux-feelings supply the *contrast set* (design language); the *pedagogy* is where the evidence lives.

### P6 — Reconstrual via storytelling  *(worked — the load-bearing bridge to Reflect)*

- **The move.** Take a whole moment apart (~week 3–4, 10–15 min, weekly-ish): observation → first/loudest feeling → what's underneath → the needs in play (often plural, sometimes in conflict) → what it clarifies. This *is* a guided journal entry (`learn.md` §3).
- **Mechanism.** Self-distancing / reconstrual (Kross & Ayduk): re-seeing an emotional episode from a **stepped-back stance** lowers reactivity and enables adaptive analysis instead of rumination. The load-bearing subtlety — **the lever is reconstrual, not writing.** Expressive writing's *average* effect is small, reliable only when the writing drives labeling, meaning-making, and reappraisal. So the storytelling structure (separate observation from evaluation; name plural/competing needs) is **engineered to force the re-see** — it's the scaffolding that turns recounting into reconstrual. The design bet is *not* "journaling helps"; it's "this specific decomposition produces the stepped-back re-see."
- **System / coupling.** Top of the spiral; operates on whole moments, not the momentary loop. Downstream of P2–P5 (needs the person able to attend, name, couple, and — critically — separate observation from judgment, which is P5 at scale). It **is** a guided Reflect entry: "not graduating *to* Reflect, walking through a door you're already standing in." This is the integration bet **H6b** — a *deferred* integration/ordering input, **not a Module-1 build gate** (decision log, 2026-07-23).
- **Preconditions.** The lower processes working well enough that a whole-moment decomposition is **tractable** (foundation §4). Time and space (10–15 min, weekly — not the 3-min loop). A real, significant-enough moment worth taking apart.
- **Failure modes.** (a) **Recounting instead of reconstruing** → the moment is retold, not re-seen (the expressive-writing small-effect trap) — *the* failure mode to design against; a pass that just vents has failed even if it's long and heartfelt. (b) Parking in "why do I always…" → rumination (violates "naming is not analyzing," §5 — reconciled below). (c) Confusing "graduating to Reflect" with "finishing Module 1" — it's a door, not a finish line.
- **Observable signals.** A genuine **reframe / new angle** on the moment vs. repeating what they already felt — this is exactly **H6b's coding target**; can separate observation from evaluation; surfaces more than one need, including needs in tension; finds the passes *tractable* enough to "do real work" (foundation §4). The operational contrast is in the deep-dive below.
- **Dials.** How much structure the decomposition gets; cadence (weekly-ish); prompt design that *forces* re-seeing vs. permits recounting; whether the vantage shift is stated explicitly or left implicit; when to introduce it (~week 3–4).
- **Evidence & grade.** Self-distancing / reconstrual — **moderate–strong, load-bearing** (`evidence-summary` §5). The defense must be *reconstrual*, never "writing helps" — the latter is weak and would misplace the mechanism. H6b tracks whether reconstrual is the lever; deferred integration input.

**▸ The recount↔reconstrue line — the operational crux.** *(Extra depth here because P6 is the load-bearing process and the one place the mechanism is easiest to fake: recounting and reconstruing look identical on the surface — both are "the person writing about a hard moment" — but they are opposite mechanisms, and the design has to drive one and starve the other.)*

| | Recounting (the failure) | Reconstruing (the target) |
|---|---|---|
| **Vantage** | first-person, re-immersed — *reliving* it | stepped-back — a fly-on-the-wall "why did this happen" |
| **Motion** | recursive: "and then I felt… and then…" | re-descriptive: what it *means*, what's underneath |
| **Self-focus** | egocentric; affect re-activated | de-centered; affect down-regulated |
| **Ending** | ends where it began, or more upset | ends with a **delta** — an angle it didn't start with |

Why the mechanism works: psychological distance de-centers the person from the egocentric immersion that drives recursive self-focus, which is what lets analysis become insight rather than rumination. What *forces* the re-see — the load-bearing design levers, each a distancing move: **(1)** the **observation-vs-evaluation split** pulls the person out of the fused "what happened = my read of it" (P5 at whole-moment scale — the entry to distance); **(2)** an explicit **vantage shift** in the prompt (stepped-back / third-person "why"), *not* just "write about it" — this is the actual Kross lever, and omitting it is what lets a journal default to venting; **(3)** surfacing **plural / competing needs** — you cannot stay immersed in one grievance while mapping a conflict of two needs, so this step *is* a reconstrual; **(4)** the closing **"what it clarifies"** demands a delta and *is* the built-in check — a pass that can't name what changed hasn't reconstrued.

**Reconciles a canon tension.** "Naming is not analyzing" (`learn.md` §5) reads as if it forbids the analysis P6 requires. It doesn't: the Kross finding is that **distance is what flips analysis from harmful to helpful** — immersed "why am I like this" (up close) is rumination; distanced "why did this happen" (stepped back) is insight. Reconstrual is therefore the *one* form of analysis the module permits, licensed only because the vantage shift makes it safe. That seam — analysis-at-distance — is precisely the boundary between Learn and Reflect.

### P7 — Loop self-initiation  *(worked — the exit condition, not a step)*

- **The move.** Not something the user performs — a **state the system reaches**: the loop runs unprompted, at least sometimes, out in life, without the app cueing it (`learn.md` / foundation §3, the meta outcome).
- **Mechanism.** Habit formation + **scaffold fading** (the worked-example → completion → independent progression): the cue that once came from the app **transfers to a life context**, and the loop becomes self-cueing. The spiral design means the loop never retires — it's a lifelong micro-habit — so self-initiation is that habit becoming self-triggering. Affect labeling being **low-effort / non-effortful** (`evidence-summary` §5) is what makes an unprompted micro-habit plausible: it doesn't spend willpower each time.
- **System / coupling.** The **exit condition of the whole spiral**, downstream of all. It both reinforces and is reinforced by P1 — running the loop unprompted is the ultimate "emotion is workable" data point. Not a tier the person does; the graduation state. Hands off to lived practice (and, later, Reflect-readiness).
- **Preconditions.** The loop must be **cheap and portable** — P2–P4 compressed into a ~3-min move that works without the app. Enough successful *prompted* reps that the pattern is grooved. A life cue capable of triggering it.
- **Failure modes.** (a) **Manufacturing self-initiation with streaks/reminders** → contradicts the no-streaks refusal *and* makes the app the cue (overjustification: remove the marker and the reason to act is gone — decision log, 2026-07-29). (b) Measuring it in a way that turns it into a performance metric. (c) Treating graduation as "done" and exiting the practice — it's meant to be lifelong.
- **Observable signals.** Reports running the loop on their own, in life, unprompted; reaches for words without the palette; the app's prompts get leaned on less over time. The clearest graduation signal and the deepest outcome — but *how* to detect it without creating a streak is a real conflict, worked through in the deep-dive below.
- **Dials.** Pace of scaffold-fading (how fast prompts withdraw); whether/how graduation is surfaced (see the resolution below); whether any check-in exists, and — crucially — whether it feeds the *settings layer* or the *user's* feedback (only the former is safe).
- **Evidence & grade.** Habit formation / scaffold fading — **practitioner-strong, not separately graded** in `evidence-summary`; the affect-labeling-is-low-effort finding (strong) underwrites the micro-habit's plausibility. The live tension is with the **no-streaks constraint** — design around it, don't measure through it.

**▸ The measurement tension, resolved.** *(Extra depth here because "instrument self-initiation" collides head-on with the no-streaks refusal — and the collision is mechanistic, not just a brand rule.)*

The trap has two independent teeth. **(1) Overjustification bites hardest exactly here.** Extrinsic markers corrode intrinsic motivation *worst for the already-motivated* (`evidence-summary` §2) — and self-initiation *is* the intrinsically-motivated state. A counter on P7 is therefore maximally corrosive: it can destroy the very outcome it measures. **(2) Counting re-introduces the app as the cue.** An unprompted micro-habit you tally in the app is no longer unprompted — the tally becomes the prompt, negating self-initiation. Measuring it can *un-graduate* the person. So P7 is the single worst place in the product to hang a metric.

The resolution — **now a recorded decision** (`../../decisions/decision-log.md`, 2026-08-01, extending the 2026-07-29 progress-indication decision and binding Reflect too) — each part tested against the function test (*remove the marker; does the reason to act survive?*):

- **Detect, don't count.** Graduation is a **one-time, capability-framed state change** ("this runs on its own now — the door to Reflect is open"), never a running total. A door you've walked through can't be *lost* — which also sidesteps the streak-cliff.
- **Infer from prompt-withdrawal + content, not a self-init tally.** The app **fades its own prompts** (the real P7 dial); continued engagement with fewer prompts, plus **qualitative mentions in storytelling** ("I caught myself doing the loop in the meeting"), are the evidence — in the content, not a scoreboard.
- **Optional opt-in self-report, surfaced once.** If the person *wants* to note an unprompted run, fine — it accrues toward the single graduation moment, not a maintained streak.
- **Any check-in feeds the settings layer, not the user.** "Has the loop shown up on its own lately?" tunes *how fast to fade prompts*; it is never a performance number shown back.
- **Accept partial unobservability.** "Unprompted, in life" is *definitionally* partly invisible to the app; forcing it to be fully observable is exactly what manufactures the streak. The module's own framing licenses this — graduation is a door, not a score.

Each part passes the function test: remove it and the person still runs the loop — because they were already running it unprompted, which is what triggered the detection in the first place.

### P8 — Noticing-under-load / situational regulation  *(future sibling — needs its own discovery)*

*Worked to the same anatomy so the mechanism is ready — but this is a **future sibling module**, and everything below its "move" is **provisional until its own discovery grounds it.** Analysed here only so it isn't quietly assumed into Module 1.*

- **The move (of the eventual module).** Notice-and-name under the conditions that *suppress* noticing — around someone you're scared of, in charged social settings, under acute stress. The distinct capability: **maintaining or recovering** interoceptive access and accurate naming when threat/appease drives are loud. Your ability to even notice what you feel is **not constant**: alone-and-calm ≠ with-someone-safe ≠ around-someone-threatening.
- **Mechanism (candidate — to be grounded).** Acute stress narrows attention and biases appraisal; competing motivational systems (threat response, the affiliative urge to appease) compete for the very attentional resource P2's interoceptive noticing needs — so the signal vanishes or is drowned. This is **regulation / capacity-under-load**, a *different construct* from vocabulary or granularity.
- **System / coupling.** **Orthogonal to Module 1** — it modulates *whether P2 can fire at all.* That is precisely why it's a **sibling module, not a Module-1 tier**: it adds no word and no distinction; it addresses the *conditions under which the whole loop is available.* A capacity here would **restore access** to the Module-1 loop in hard contexts. Add it *next to* Module 1, never by carving capacity *out of* it.
- **Preconditions (for building it).** Its **own Phase-1 discovery** — the current Module-1 interviews do not probe social/threat modulation at all. Module 1's loop should be reasonably installed first (you can't regulate-under-load a loop you can't yet run when calm).
- **Failure modes (of the concept — to guard now).** (a) Folding it into Module 1 and thereby re-narrowing or bloating it (the thing we just walled off). (b) Assuming Module-1 discovery covers it. (c) Sliding from "noticing under load" into **trauma/clinical territory** — the discovery ethics ("don't probe trauma," Doc 1 §8) bind *harder* here; this likely has a boundary with professional support that must be drawn before build.
- **Observable signals (candidate).** The alone-vs-safe-vs-threatening **gradient** in a person's own accounts — where noticing holds vs. collapses; whether they can *recover* naming after a spike. To be validated by its discovery.
- **Dials.** TBD by its own design — not yet ours to set.
- **Evidence & grade.** **Not yet assembled.** Stress-driven attention-narrowing and competing-motivation accounts are plausible anchors, but this needs its own evidence pass, as Module 1 got. "Resilience" is a candidate lineage but a *loose* term — **pin the actual mechanism before committing.** Flag: ungraded.
