- **`root-website-versioning-and-admin.md`** — spec, **partly built**, extending v3: **independent version control** for the contract and the design (two immutable revision lineages), a **signed contract frozen with amendments**, **design carry-forward** of unchanged approvals, and the **full admin dashboard** that authors them. All three open decisions now closed — **admin-only authorship** (the tracked "revision requested" state deferred as a named refactor) and **free-text amendments**, with structured supersede-Article-N rejected rather than deferred, because it would make the readable contract a *computed* document. *(0.2 · 2026-08-04.)*
- **`root-website-research-lab.md`** — spec (not built): the **Research Lab**, a public bilingual library of referenced + own research under the **Library** section (opens the Root Cast content lock; one strand of the Library, beside Root Cast). Concept tree + list + search, translation provenance, public/private visibility, admin + contributor curation, agent last. Umbrella label locked **Library / کتابخانه** at `/:lang/library`. Its last two open items closed 2026-08-04, and neither was what it had been filed as: the contributor/admin "permission line" is a **capability model over a role set**, and the "per-source rights workflow" is a **required rights field with no default** — which also turned out to be the agent's quote-versus-cite scope, unblocking R4. *(0.3 · 2026-08-04.)*

Records the 2026-08-01 sequencing: versioning to depth first, the Library second, a live client stood down, demo mode retired, local-disk storage — and, from 2026-08-04, the Review Room third. **F1 (upload/storage) is closed** — design files 2026-08-03, the printable contract 2026-08-04, which also settled that a contract PDF is a *rendering* of a published revision and never the record. **F3 (roles as a set, capabilities as the guard) is built as of 2026-08-05**, which makes **F2, the staff shell at `/desk`, the only unbuilt foundation — all three tracks wait on it.** 0.7 planned the dashboard to its end: track C added, **V1b** added ahead of everything because the draft is still invisible to GraphQL, **F3 rewritten** from one enum value into the app's permission model and moved *before* F2, and every blocking open decision closed. Read this before starting any of the three. *(0.8 · 2026-08-05.)*
- **`root-website-review-room.md`** — **spec** as of 2026-08-04, and sequenced by the build plan as track C: a private panel where an invited specialist (psychologist, sociologist, manager) reads **Root's own documents** and **leaves comments**. Sibling to the Research Lab and opposite in every property that matters — private not public, our documents not others', and the reader writes. Its three deciding questions are answered: **snapshot at review time** (and the freeze is a git commit), **one corpus for everyone defined by an allowlist** — which reverses the document's own per-reviewer-grant reading — and **passage-level anchoring**, which the snapshot is what makes safe. *(0.2 · 2026-08-04.)*
