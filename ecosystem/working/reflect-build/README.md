# Reflect Build — workstream

The **build** track for the Reflect pillar (`../../canon/02-pillars/reflect.md`). Living layer.

Act 1 of the pillar does one thing first: **re-seeing a single moment** — the reconstrual lever `reflect.md` §6 names as what makes reflective writing pay off, turned into a tool. The tool is **Re-seeing** *(working name)*: tell one moment, mark what was your read rather than what happened, look at the **camera version** of your own story, step back, name what's underneath, and say what you see now.

Where the pillar canon says *what Reflect is*, this track answers *what we build first, in what shape, and why not the obvious shape.*

**Contents:**
1. **`00-act1-concept.md`** — the concept and its grounding. Why a journal with prompts and an AI mirror are both ruled out as the first build, the levers graded, a category map against journals, mood trackers, thought records and AI journaling, the frame and the six-move sitting, the camera version as a model-free mirror, the hard fences, third-party data, the seams with Learn Module 1 and Impact, and the disconfirmers written before the data. **v0.1 (2026-09-30).**

**Settled inputs (2026-09-30):** built as a **tool inside Tracker** later, migrating to Reflect's standalone app after that (the Feelings & Needs and Noticing arrangement); **LLM-free v1** — the mirror is built from the person's own words and the catches run on authored lexicons, with the model mirror deferred until we know whether the moves produce re-seeing at all; **Reflect's own tool, not Learn Module 1's Tier 4** — the deferred storytelling tier lands here, and Module 1's graduation can link to it; Module 1 is **not** a prerequisite (`../../canon/02-pillars/00-the-loop.md` §5). English-only prototype; the Persian tool name is open.

**Next:** the spec (processes, screen flow, dials, data model, milestones), wireframes, a spine evaluation, then the build plan — the Noticing sequence.
