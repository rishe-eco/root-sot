Reading the guardrails in the frames:
  • The person sees first. Nothing is pre-highlighted in move 2; the lexicon speaks only after “done”, about an unmarked hit, at most twice a sitting — and “it’s what happened” is accepted without argument. If the software marked the reads, it would have done the seeing.
  • The camera version is made only by removal. The software lifts out what the person marked and writes nothing; the gaps are left for the person to mend, because mending is re-description. This is the model-free mirror: their own words, sorted along the one axis the pillar cares about.
  • Reads are kept, never disputed. No evidence-for/against, no distortion labels, no “that’s not true”. Nothing’s wrong with these; they’re just not the same thing as what happened.
  • No numbers anywhere — no objectivity score, no intensity ratings, no before/after, no count of moments. The share-of-story-that-was-interpretation is the tempting one, and it isn’t computed even internally.
  • Catches are silent during telling. Checking while someone writes teaches them to write for the checker.
  • The scale floor is a filmable moment. Sprawl gets one gentle “which moment inside it?” — concrete processing is the constructive kind; abstract “what does this say about me” is the spiral.
  • The step-back prompt never asks “why do I…”. A why asked close up is rumination; the same why from the corner of the room is insight.
  • Every sitting ends moving forward — the delta line, then now-or-hand-off. “Nothing yet” and “nothing — this was enough” are both finished endings.
  • Leaving is always available, “let it go” writes nothing, only one moment can wait at a time, and where-to-get-help sits on the frame and the leave screen.
  • Other people exist only in free text. No person field, no search, no filter — the log can’t become a file on someone. Private-only; any future sharing needs de-identification first.
  • The log is a record and nothing more. No cross-entry anything: “you’ve written about work three times” is pattern recognition, which is gated, not merely later.
