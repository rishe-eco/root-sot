Reading the guardrails in the frames:
  • Scale floor — the place palette has no option larger than somewhere you physically stood, and the person step asks for one person. Compassion fades on aggregates; it doesn't on someone with a pram.
  • Observation leads inference (N4 before N5), and the need is always skippable — "not sure" closes a pass, it doesn't leave one open.
  • The small thing is optional and skipped by default, and is never marked done: recognition is the output, execution belongs to Organize.
  • The third-party warning is part of the field, not a modal. Entries are private-only, names are free text and never indexed — there is no view of a person across entries, because building one is what would make this a dossier.
  • Catches never block and never score, fire at most once a sitting, and the motive catch routes to Reflect rather than forming an opinion.
  • Capacity is read backwards from what you turned out to have — there is no skills form anywhere, and the portrait stores no names.
  • The day-one frame is a rehearsal, not a preamble — beat 1 has the person name their own unsaid need and then what was actually visible about it, so the turn is their own two answers laid side by side. They run the observation→need inference once, from the inside, before the loop ever asks for it.
  • The log refuses by design — no counts, no patterns, no missing-marks, and no search or person filter, because a log you can search by a name is a file on that person.
  • Nothing counts. No streak, no total, no impact score — the absence is in the schema, not just the UI.
