### Session 2 — Finding the fifth pillar (14 Jul) — *the canon was created here*

The big consolidation session. Produced the 16-file `root-canon/` directory and started the changelog.

**Purpose reframe:** "help you get stuff done and maintain" → "know yourself and grow" → the brand thesis above.

**The five pillars, defined:**
- **Reflect** — articulate experience, mirror it back, recognize patterns. *Immediate-action-only output.*
- **Maintain** — recognize three batteries (physical, mental, spiritual) and respond *now*. No schedules.
- **Organize** — recognize active goals, understand loopholes, track, set new goals with a **mindset-first** frame and **first-class failure handling**.
- **Learn** — build capacity intentionally; first module is emotion-and-need language as a coupled spiral, Persian-first.
- **Others** *(working name)* — need recognition: see where your capacity meets the world's need; feeds Organize via Reflect's motive check. **Name unresolved** — candidates: Contribute, Serve, Relate, خدمت.

**Architecture locked:**
- Reflect and Maintain emit **immediate actions only** — nothing deferred, no intervals, no streaks. The **overjustification effect** grounds this as empirical design, not aesthetics.
- **Reflect↔Organize coupling** (discovered in-session): Reflect audits the *motive* of service goals before they enter Organize, and owns the emotional/meaning side of failure; Organize owns the structural side (in-my-control vs. not).
- Discipline reframed as the **container for authentic service**, not its enemy.
- **Agent constraint:** it must help users *author* goals, never author *for* them — goal-setting theory's benefits require self-endorsement.

**Development engine:** five phases around the **Opportunity Solution Tree (OST)** as the single shared artifact — (1) map 3–5 opportunity nodes from discovery; (2) generate solution approaches with explicit assumptions; (3) run a lean test against the riskiest assumption; (4) one synchronous decision meeting (iterate/pivot/move on); (5) consolidate into a feature spec. The tree is the **async coordination artifact**; gate definitions double as coordination protocol. **WIP limited to one pillar in Phase 3 at a time.** This replaced the earlier 14-step checklist (folded into tree-building moves).

**Corrections made this session:**
- The "measuring changes the measured" risk was **re-assigned from Maintain to Reflect** (it concentrates in articulation). The B-10 / device-in-the-drawer concern belongs to **Organize/Today** (action deferral), not Maintain.
- Split two risks previously conflated under "streaks": the avoidable **streak-cliff** (solved by not building streaks) vs. the ambient **motivation-fade** abandonment (the genuinely hard one — deferred).
- Reflect's cross-entry **pattern recognition** downgraded from core feature to **gated**, pending professional psychological review.
- Distinguished the **team's** working language (English) from the **product's** feelings/needs language (Persian-first).

**Constraints locked:**
- **Tech:** Vite + React Router (frontend); Express + GraphQL + Prisma/SQLite (backend).
- **Bilingual:** Root/ریشه, Journey/ماجرا.
- **LLM:** **Mistral primary (EU), Qwen secondary**, with tool-layer capability limits.
- **Team:** three, remote, async-first. (Two new members being onboarded — one from dentistry/insurance, one a CS student/junior dev; both moderate English.)

**Repos inspected** (the tracker, mid-transformation toward Root):

### Session 4 — Mental health through the Bahá'í framework (15 Jul)

A philosophical/textual inquiry into where modern psychology's concept of *mental health* sits within ʻAbdu'l-Bahá's anthropological model in *Some Answered Questions* (Mofāvazāt).

**Three questions posed:** what mental health means in modern psychology and its underlying mindset; how ʻAbdu'l-Bahá's model is structured; and where mental health locates within it.

**Analysis produced:** modern psychology's negative *and* positive definitions, its methodological naturalism and smuggled normativity, and the Avicennian faculty psychology in the text. A **three-register decomposition** of mental health within the model: (1) neurophysiological instrument integrity; (2) right ordering of the inner faculties governed by reason; (3) spiritual orientation understood through privation. The **"replaceable scaffolding" question** was raised — whether the five-powers framework carries revealed weight or functions as revisable scientific vocabulary.

**Then you asked for verification against the actual text.** A systematic multi-chapter review followed (Ocean of Lights + Bahá'í Reference Library, Persian original alongside the 2014 authorized English), producing **two self-corrections:**
- The **estimative faculty (wahm)** does *not* appear in ʻAbdu'l-Bahá's five inner powers. The list is **imagination, thought, comprehension, memory, and the common faculty** — already departing from Avicenna's canonical scheme.
- The **"broken lute / madness"** passage attributed to the Mofāvazāt is **not in that book.**

**Enrichments from the review:** the bidirectional human spirit (capable of both greatest perfection and greatest deficiency) from the *Five Kinds of Spirit* chapter; an explicit psychosomatic causal channel in the healing chapters; a stated ceiling on spiritual healing overriding material illness; the two-axis subtle/palpable torment distinction from *Two Kinds of Torment*; and the *Four Criteria* chapter as an **internal warrant** for treating the model's own scaffolding as fallible and revisable.

**Status:** paused awaiting your next question, with the **replaceable-scaffolding question** confirmed as the direction to pursue.

### Session 6 — Ubuntu VPS setup (16 Jul)

Full runbook for a fresh **Ubuntu 22.04** VPS hosting **Nahal** (temporary here before migration), **Root's website**, the **tracker**, and **Hesab** — all Express/GraphQL (Apollo/Yoga) + Vite.

**Architecture — hybrid:** host **Nginx** terminates TLS (via Certbot) and serves static Vite files; all app backends run **containerized**, bound to localhost.

| Service | Local port |
|---------|-----------|
| Nahal | 8080 |
| Root | 4001 |
| Tracker | 4002 |
| Hesab | 4003 |

Nahal is containerized specifically so its eventual migration is trivial — the whole site travels as named volumes + a compose file. **Hardware:** 8 GB RAM, 3 CPU cores (a 4th under consideration).

**The SSH lockout incident (and its resolution — worth keeping):**
- **Cause:** root login and password auth were disabled before key auth was properly verified. The SSH agent was offering only GitHub keys, triggering `Too many authentication failures` before the correct deploy key was reached.
- **Fix:** `ssh -o IdentitiesOnly=yes -i ~/.ssh/vps_deploy deploy@<VPS_IP>` to bypass agent interference, then permanently pin the key in `~/.ssh/config` with `IdentitiesOnly yes`.
- **Secondary issue:** `ssh-keygen` failing with "No such file or directory" — tilde expansion doesn't work outside Git Bash on Windows; run it in Git Bash. Also `authorized_keys` was empty/mis-populated from the rsync step.
- **Verification method locked (your preference):** layered — structural check + fingerprint match (`ssh-keygen -lf` local vs. server) + `auth.log` `Accepted publickey` as the *unfakeable* receipt.

**AFFiNE added to the stack:** fits the same Compose-behind-Nginx pattern, with **WebSocket upgrade headers** added to the Nginx block. 8 GB RAM is sufficient; **CPU contention on 3 cores is the soft constraint** — recommendation: add a 4th core and apply `cpus` limits on bursty containers to protect AFFiNE's latency-sensitive real-time sync. Full walkthrough covered: compose download, `.env` (critical pre-start vars), localhost binding, the Nginx WebSocket block, admin account creation, and backup strategy.

**Also addressed:** serving static HTML/CSS/JS — three options ranked (dedicated subdomain *recommended*; subpath; raw IP HTTP-only, discouraged). And a side question on Android emulators for real apps with Google sign-in (Google Play Games on PC, Genymotion, BlueStacks; Play Integrity caution for certain app categories).

## 5. What we learned

**Research (product-grounding):** SDT and the overjustification effect are strong and load-bearing (they justify immediate-action-only + no-streaks). Goal-setting theory is strong but has a documented dark side — authored-goal invalidity — hence the agent-authors-nothing rule. Personal-informatics literature: motivation loss is the primary cause of *permanent* dropout. Affect labeling is strong but **language-dependent** — no regulation in a non-dominant language — which makes Persian-first a constraint, not a preference. Emotional granularity and malleability mindset: moderate-to-strong. NVC: thin evidence — a design language only, though its component moves have independent support. Self-distancing (Kross & Ayduk): reconstrual, not mere writing, is the active lever (H6b).

**Textual (Bahá'í track):** ʻAbdu'l-Bahá's five inner powers are imagination, thought, comprehension, memory, common faculty — *not* Avicenna's scheme, and no wahm. The "broken lute" passage isn't in the Mofāvazāt. The *Four Criteria* chapter licenses treating the model's own scaffolding as revisable.

**Technical (hard-won, worth keeping):**
- SSH: pin keys with `IdentitiesOnly yes`; agent key-spraying causes `Too many authentication failures`; verify with fingerprint match + `auth.log` `Accepted publickey`.
- Windows: `~` only expands in Git Bash for `ssh-keygen`.
- AFFiNE: needs WebSocket upgrade headers in Nginx; CPU (not RAM) is the constraint on 3 cores.
- PDF: empty `pdftotext` + no `pdffonts` = rasterized PDF → render with `pdftoppm -jpeg -r 150`.
- `docx` (Node) Persian RTL: `rightToLeft: true` on every `TextRun`, `bidirectional: true` + `AlignmentType.RIGHT` on every `Paragraph`; substitute Persian digits programmatically; `cantSplit: true` on the `TableRow` (not the Table); QA in LibreOffice via Vazirmatn + a fontconfig Tahoma→Vazirmatn alias.
